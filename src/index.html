Chek on the web

And in any browser.

Sign in to your Chek server at app.chek.page and pick up where your iPhone left off. Comics, EPUB, PDF, text and Markdown, your shelves and your sites, on any computer.

  • Your place saves to your server, so every device opens on the same page.
  • Install it from the browser, and it opens like an app.
  • Every Chek server serves it at its own address, with an admin panel at /admin.
