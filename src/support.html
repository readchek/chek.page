Support

Something not working, or a question? Write to support@chek.page. Tell us your Chek version, from Settings, and what you were doing. If you have a GitHub account, you can also open an issue.

Adding books

Open a book from the Files app or the share sheet and choose Chek, or tap Add Books in the Library tab. Chek reads EPUB, PDF, CBZ, CBT, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, plain text and Markdown files.

Connecting a server

In Settings, tap Connect a Server and enter its address with your name and password. Chek connects to Chek servers, which can also serve your Komga libraries. If the server is on your home network, allow Chek to use the local network when iOS asks.

The demo

Tap Try the Demo to read sample books and comics from demo.chek.page without setting anything up. The demo ends when you close Chek, and nothing from it is kept.
